**Status:** concrete role list; no endpoint scoring; not an activated sample

This manifest is the first concrete Paper 11 galaxy-role list. It imports
source-side roles from existing Tau Core galaxy-readout work without importing
endpoint scores or family verdicts.

## What Is Frozen

The manifest freezes four role groups:

- source-observable train/formula candidates;
- weak/null and rejection controls;
- warp/history and high-spin challenge cases;
- beta-closure transfer candidates.

The list is intentionally role-based. It does not yet activate endpoint
scoring and it does not decide whether `M0`, `M1`, or `M2` is favored.

## Claim Boundary

This manifest does not claim:

- a final statistical sample;
- a Paper 11 held-out score;
- that Tau Core explains any listed galaxy;
- that a dark-sector response is needed or unnecessary.
